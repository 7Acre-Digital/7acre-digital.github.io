Refund & Cancellation Policy

30-day happiness guarantee

If you're not happy with your website for any reason within 30 days after launch, tell me by email or text and I'll refund everything you've paid 7Acre Digital (setup fee and any monthly payments). I'll then take the site down and end the agreement.

  • Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe, normally within 5–10 business days.
  • The guarantee doesn't cover money paid to other companies (for example your domain registrar or Google Workspace). Those accounts are yours to keep.
  • After the first 30 days, setup fees and past monthly payments are not refundable, but you can cancel future months as described below.

Monthly plans and cancellation

  • Starter, Standard, and Premium: 3-month minimum, then month-to-month. The minimum doesn't apply if you use the 30-day guarantee.
  • $0-down plan: 12-month minimum, then month-to-month. To end early, pay a one-time $497 buyout instead of the remaining months.
  • To cancel: give 30 days' notice by email or text, anytime. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the notice period (or the end of the minimum term, if later), and future charges stop then. There is no cancellation fee.
  • When you cancel, hosting stops and I'll point your domain wherever you'd like or hand it back to you.
